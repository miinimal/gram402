Your AI agent pays for itself. Never more than you allowed.

An agent hits a paid API and gets HTTP 402. Instead of stopping for a human, it pays from a PayPal mandate you approved once, or with GRAM, and gets the answer with a signed receipt. Every limit is enforced by the server, not by the model.

How it works

1 · MandateThe owner sets a budget, a cap per request, an expiry and allowed endpoints, and approves once on PayPal (Vault v3).
2 · 402The agent calls a paid endpoint and receives an x402 offer: pay with GRAM or from the PayPal mandate.
3 · PayThe server checks every limit, reserves the amount and captures a PayPal order with an idempotency key.
4 · ReceiptThe answer comes with an Ed25519-signed receipt bound to the PayPal capture id. Revoke at any moment.

One 402, two rails

paypal-mandate

For agents whose owner pays with PayPal. Limits are held by the server; the model never sees the PayPal account.

ton-transfer

For agents with a TON wallet: a plain GRAM transfer with the invoice comment, verified on chain, no facilitator.
